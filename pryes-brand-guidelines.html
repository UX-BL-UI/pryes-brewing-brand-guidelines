01Brand Foundation

Who we are, before we say a word.

Pryes is setting the new standard for Minnesota craft beer. A large variety — style-defining sours, Midwest IPAs, classic pilsners and more — intentionally brewed and consistently excellent. Easy to love, even easier to drink. No gimmicks, no guesswork. We’re in it because we want every Minnesotan to find their favorite beer.

Audience

Pryes attracts a wide range of people, and that’s one of our greatest strengths. We’re inclusive, welcoming and have mass appeal. Defining our core audience isn’t about narrowing who we serve — it’s about understanding the people most likely to choose Pryes again and again.

Audience Basics

  • Men and women; ~25–60
  • Twin Cities metro area
  • Upper-middle to high income
  • Mostly novice-to-intermediate craft drinkers

Why they choose Pryes

  • A trusted friend or pro recommended it.
  • Packaging feels approachable, elevated and helpful — recognizable styles and flavors.
  • A broad, well-stocked shelf signals legitimacy and reliability.
  • “By MN, for MN” resonates — the quality Minnesota choice, not the small local compromise.
  • They can be proud to bring it anywhere.
We Are / We Are Not

We Are

  • Setting standards
  • Intentional craft & consistent quality
  • Approachable beer for every taste
  • Easy afternoons & chill nights
  • Friendly, familiar & mid-range $$$
  • Warm, easygoing & unpretentiously educational
  • Locally loved & unapologetically Minnesotan
  • A professional operation with big ambitions

We Are Not

  • Chasing trends
  • Hit-or-miss experiments or mass-produced meh
  • Craft snob territory
  • Wild parties & shots at the bar
  • Buttoned-up, exclusive, $$$$$
  • Cool, gate-kept or “figure it out yourself”
  • A Minnesotan caricature or tourist trap
  • A scrappy upstart underdog (not anymore)
Brand Pillars
01

Approachable Craft

We take the craft seriously but keep the experience easy. From flavor graphs to friendly taproom service, exploring craft beer should feel comfortable, not complicated.

02

By Minnesotans, For Minnesotans

The largest Minnesota-only brewery, by design. While others go wide, we go deep — staying here, self-distributing, making beer this state can be proud of.

03

Intentional Quality

We don’t chase trends or cut corners. We brew with intention and only release beers that meet our standards. You can always trust you’re getting the highest quality.

04

A Beer for Everyone

Easy drinkers, hazy IPAs, bright sours, rich stouts, NA options and more. Whatever your taste, there’s a Pryes beer for you — and we’ll help you find your favorite.

Positioning

We’re just as ambitious as Surly or Bell’s, but in a more Minnesota-centric way. We’re more approachable and less niche than brands like Modist and Indeed, and a potential entry point for emerging craft drinkers. More modern than Summit — with the potential to be just as timeless.

In the Twin Cities market, we have more variety on shelf and larger distribution than most craft breweries, local or regional.

Pryes is

  • The brewery with a beer for everyone
  • For Minnesotans, by Minnesotans (think: the New Glarus of Minnesota)
  • The enjoy-it-anywhere beer that’s never a gamble
Voice

Our voice is warm, confident and refreshingly down-to-earth. We take brewing seriously, but we don’t take ourselves too seriously. We know our craft deeply, but we talk like humans, not beer textbooks — clear, familiar language that makes everyone feel welcome.

Elevated, but approachable. Professional, but never dull. Smart, but never snobbish. Just real people who love what we do.

Our voice is not

Writing Guidelines
  1. Keep it simple. Use active voice and familiar words. Get to the point fast — even if you add a few bonus thoughts (like this) later.
  2. Include everyone. Follow inclusive and accessibility standards. Keep pop-culture references, generational slang and internet speak to a minimum.
  3. Use punctuation well. Oxford comma. Commas in numbers over three digits (5,000). Exclamation points and ALL CAPS sparingly — we rarely get that excited. Spaces around em dashes ( — ). Ampersands only in headings, never in sentences. Emojis in social only, and one is almost always enough.

Clear Space & Minimum Size

Clear Space

Give the logo room to breathe. Keep a perimeter of roughly half the wordmark’s height clear of other elements. For the crest, base the margin on ¼ the height of the coin.

04Typography

Slab-serif headlines, easygoing sans.

A Clarendon-style serif does the talking; a geometric sans handles the details; a tall condensed face carries the all-caps subheads. Together they read confident, warm and unmistakably Pryes.

A note on web type

The core brand fonts — Superclarendon, Gotham and Alternate Gothic No. 2 D — are licensed for print and design tools. On the web we use open-source stand-ins chosen to match their character: Bitter, a Clarendon-style slab, stands in for Superclarendon, with Poppins for Gotham and Barlow Condensed for Alternate Gothic.

05Photography

Shot like a friend brought their old camera.

A balanced mix of clean lifestyle scenes and product heroes in familiar Midwestern settings — tailgating, patio, lakeside. Approachable, everyday, trusted. Slightly filmic, never fussy.

Approachable
Warm, relatable, unpretentious.
Everyday
Casual gatherings, real moments.
Trusted
Consistent, quality, dependable.
Minnesota
Midwest lifestyle, by MN for MN.
Direction

Lifestyle first. Wider images with multiple people, props and settings, plus product heroes inside lifestyle environments. Scenes should feel captured, not staged.

Textural backdrops. For social, lifestyle or textural photography can sit behind product overlays — a simple execution that ties product to experience and translates well to motion.

Avoid glassware. Feature packaging and detail shots of the beer’s texture — foam, bubbles — rather than reflective, hard-to-shoot glasses.

Filmic treatment. Edit stock to look film-esque: add noise scaled to image size, soften whites and blacks slightly, and lean on VSCO / Lightroom presets like Kodak Chroma or Portra.
