Components

Buttons

Tuned for touch: a 44px tap target for primary actions and generous horizontal padding, so buttons read a little wider than they are tall. Small and extra-small sizes (36 and 32px) are for dense or secondary UI. Every variant keeps a visible, offset focus ring.
